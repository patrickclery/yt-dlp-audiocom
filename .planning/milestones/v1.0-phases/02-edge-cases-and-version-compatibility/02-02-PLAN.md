## Acceptance Criteria (this plan, expansion slices)

- [ ] A developer can re-record any track's fixture with its download flag and original-upload metadata, still provably free of signed URLs and uploader filenames (enables SC1 / EXTR-04 offline)
- [ ] A re-uploaded track lists only its current upload's formats and reports its real duration, on both versions ("works on every track variant", D-11)

<objective>
Two expansion slices on top of the 02-01 tracer. (1) The fixture recorder and the 12 committed fixtures capture what EXTR-04 needs offline (is_downloadable, is_public, sources[], transcodings[].source_id) without leaking the signed source URLs or the uploader's original filenames. (2) Users downloading a re-uploaded track get only the current upload, with its real duration, instead of stale duplicate formats and the old duration.

Purpose: data foundation for the EXTR-04 gate in 02-03, delivered as a real fix (D-11, RESEARCH Pattern 1, Open Question 3), verified on both versions (PLUG-02). This plan also picks the current source, which is the one 02-03 offers as the original.

Output: an extended `scripts/record_fixture.py` and `tests/test_fixture_hygiene.py`, 12 fixtures, the `current_transcodings` helper in `tests/conftest.py`, updated tests, and newest-source selection in `yt_dlp_plugins/extractor/audiocom.py`.
</objective>
